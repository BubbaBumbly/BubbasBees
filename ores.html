Ores and comb drop rates

Every ore block every ore bee can make, with its comb drop rate.

How ore combs work. Ore combs craft only ore blocks, never the ore's item: 8 ore combs around 1 ore block (the seed, used up) make 2 of that ore block, so each craft gains one block. The seed picks the variant (stone, deepslate or Nether). Mine the blocks with a Fortune pickaxe for more.
Comb drop rate is the chance that a trip brings home the bee's comb. Ore bees work every trip except the diamond bee (1 in 4) and the ancient debris bee (1 in 6); a failed roll brings home nothing. A full busy hive makes about 210 trips per hour of work.

Reading the table

  • Hive-hour = one hour of work for a full busy hive of 5 bees of that kind, in its home dimension. Two hives make twice as much.
  • Real hour counts the night: Overworld ore bees work only in daytime (10.9 of every 20 real minutes) and stay in when it rains, so a real hour holds about 55% of a hive-hour. The quartz and ancient debris bees live in the Nether, where there is no night.
  • Away from home (a quartz or ancient debris hive in the Overworld, or an Overworld ore bee in the Nether), hives keep their bees twice as long: about 123 trips per hive-hour instead of 210.
  • Mined columns are averages from the vanilla loot tables: Fortune III gives about 2.2 times the drops of every ore except redstone, which gets about 1.5 more dust per block. Ancient debris always drops itself (smelt it for netherite scrap: 4 scrap + 4 gold make an ingot).
  • The ore bees' gems need the Celestial Bubba Star.
