How the rates work

The numbers in this guide come from the mod's own files and from timing full hives in a test world.

Trips

  • A full busy hive (5 bees of one kind) made 210 ± 1.9 trips per hour of work, the same in the Overworld (day), the Nether and at night for starlight bees. A trip is about 86 seconds per bee: 60 inside the hive and 26 out at flowers.
  • Away from home the hive keeps its bees twice as long: about 123 trips per hive-hour.
  • Boosted (home biome or the royal queen nearby) the hive keeps them 20% shorter: about 244 trips per hive-hour (worked out, not timed).
  • Calm hives are slower: their bees wander and stay inside twice as long.

Combs

Combs per hive-hour = trips × the chance a trip brings that comb home. Rare combs roll first on every trip; if they miss, the bee's own comb rolls (100% for most bees). The river bee splits its trips half and half between its two combs.

Crafts

Items per hive-hour = combs per hive-hour ÷ combs per craft × what the craft gains (a seed that comes back out doesn't count). When a recipe needs two kinds of comb, the slower kind sets the pace, with one hive of each.

Working hours

A Minecraft day is 20 real minutes: about 10.9 minutes of day and 9.1 of night. Overworld bees work in daytime and dry weather, starlight bees at night, and bees in the Nether and the End all the time. Multiply hive-hours by the working share to get real hours.
