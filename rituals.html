Rituals

Two rituals, both a race to make bees pollinate inside a circle before time runs out. A boss bar shows the progress and what's still missing. If a ritual fails, the progress is lost but the item on the altar is safe. Hives inside the circle make no combs while a ritual runs.

The Celestial Ritual

Turns a Bubba Star into a Celestial Bubba Star, which every ore bee's gem needs. It lasts one night, under open sky.

Celestial Altar holding the Bubba Star

Stardust: one exact ring, radius 8 (56 pieces)

End rods, 8 blocks out at north, east, south and west

4 pedestals 6 out and 6 across, each holding an echo shard

12 candles, any colour: 4 next to the altar, 2 within 2 blocks of each pedestal (inside the ring). The spots shown are one way to place them.

The ritual area: everything inside the ring, from 4 below the altar to 8 above.

Everything sits on the altar's layer.

To win, before sunrise

  • 100 pollinations inside the circle,
  • 50 of them by starlight bees,
  • and at least one each by these 8 bees: Glow Bee, Amethyst Bee, Deep Dark Bee, Obsidian Bee, Soul Bee, Prismarine Bee, Frost Bee, Corrupt Nature Bee.

A pollination counts when a bee from a hive inside the circle finishes a flower visit inside it. During the ritual those hives send their bees out at night, even bees that normally sleep.

Starting

Build the circle, put the Bubba Star on the altar and light every candle, at night. Whichever you do last starts the ritual. If something is missing, the game tells you what and where.

Dark bees

Every 30 to 60 seconds a dark bee appears somewhere on the ring and flies to a candle (about 5 seconds) to snuff it out. While a candle is out the ritual is paused and pollinations don't count: relight it. One hit kills a dark bee; royal guards attack them on sight. In the last minute before sunrise they come two at a time.

It fails if

  • the circle is broken or the star is taken off the altar,
  • everyone leaves (the area unloads),
  • a bee from the circle is killed,
  • or the sun rises.
Tip: bring 4 starlight hives (20 bees). In testing, 3 hives won with about 3 minutes to spare out of a 9.1-minute night, and one candle left out for a few minutes nearly cost the ritual. Put one hive of each of the 8 bees above inside the circle too, with their flowers, and keep a sword handy for dark bees.

The Royal Summoning

Summons the royal queen. Easier and cheaper than the Celestial Ritual, and done in daytime.

Celestial Altar holding a golden helmet (the crown; not used up)

The altar stands on a 3×3 of honeycomb blocks with gold block corners

Potted flowers 6 out at north, east, south and west

Potted flowers 4 out and 4 across, standing on honey blocks

The ritual area, radius 6.

  • Start: put the golden helmet on the altar in daytime with the structure complete.
  • Win: 40 pollinations inside the circle within 5 minutes, any bees, including at least 2 by royal guards.
  • It can't start while a royal queen is alive. It fails the same ways as the Celestial Ritual (or when the 5 minutes run out).

About 12 bees in 3 hives, one or two of them royal guards, is enough. See the royal queen for what she does.

Building with the Encyclobeedia

Both rituals have a page in the Encyclobeedia with the layout in 3D, slowly turning. The eye button under it shows the layout in your world as a see-through ghost of the blocks still missing.

  • Until you pin it, the ghost follows the block you look at and snaps onto an altar. Right-click to pin it there.
  • Blocks in the way that don't belong get a red outline.
  • A counter shows how many blocks are in place out of the total (77 for the Celestial Ritual, 22 for the Royal Summoning). Candles count anywhere they'd work for the ritual, not only on the spots shown.
  • Clear it with the eye button again, or sneak and right-click with an empty hand.
